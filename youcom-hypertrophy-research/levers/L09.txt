LEVER: set structure. Methods: drop sets, rest-pause, myo-reps, supersets (agonist-antagonist vs same-muscle/compound sets), cluster sets - each vs traditional straight sets.
Score each method as its own impact_scoring entry (scope="sub_method"); use the lever_overall entry for the overall verdict.
