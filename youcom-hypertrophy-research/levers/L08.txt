RUN: L08 - Exercise selection and stability
LEVER: choice of exercises. Comparison: machines/cables vs free weights; stability and local muscle loading; resistance profiles (cable/lever vs free weight); single-joint vs multi-joint; exercise variation for regional hypertrophy.
Sub-methods for impact_scoring: machines vs free weights; single- vs multi-joint; exercise variation.
Questions:
a) Machines vs free weights for whole-muscle and regional hypertrophy.
b) Do single-joint exercises add hypertrophy beyond multi-joint exercises, and for which muscles?
c) Non-uniform (regional) hypertrophy across exercises and the value of exercise variation.
d) Resistance-profile/strength-curve matching: longitudinal evidence, or only theoretical?
e) Untrained vs trained.
