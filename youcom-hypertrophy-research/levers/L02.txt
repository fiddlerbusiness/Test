RUN: L02 - Range of motion and muscle length (stretch-mediated hypertrophy)
LEVER: range of motion and the muscle length at which tension is applied. Comparison: training at long muscle lengths; lengthened partials vs full ROM vs shortened partials.
Keep two claims strictly separate and score each as its own sub_method entry: (A) "lengthened partials beat full ROM"; (B) "include the stretched (long-length) position", i.e. full ROM or long-length partials vs short-length partials.
Questions:
a) Whole-muscle vs regional (proximal/distal) hypertrophy.
b) Exercise-specific evidence: seated vs prone leg curl; overhead vs neutral-arm elbow extension; knee-flexion depth in leg press/squat; calf raises.
c) Untrained vs trained.
d) Reconcile positive single studies with null or contrary results and with newer meta-analyses on mean muscle length and regional hypertrophy.
e) Longitudinal growth (fascicle length) vs cross-sectional growth: what is measured, and does it change the conclusion for the primary outcome?
