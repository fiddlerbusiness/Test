RUN: L06 - Repetition tempo and eccentric control
LEVER: repetition tempo. Comparison: total repetition duration (~0.5-8 s vs >10 s); eccentric duration (~1 s vs 2-4 s) vs intentional maximal concentric velocity; eccentric vs concentric emphasis.
Separate tempo per se from its confounds: tempo changes the usable load, the repetitions completed and the proximity to failure. For each trial, state which of these were controlled.
Sub-methods for impact_scoring: total repetition duration; eccentric duration; eccentric vs concentric emphasis.
Questions:
a) Is there a range of repetition durations within which hypertrophy is equivalent, and where does it break down?
b) Does a slower eccentric add hypertrophy when load and proximity to failure are matched?
c) Untrained vs trained.
