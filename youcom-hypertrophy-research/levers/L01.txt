RUN: L01 - Mechanical tension and proximity to failure
LEVER: proximity to failure of each set. Comparison: sets ended at momentary muscular failure (0 RIR) vs 1-3 RIR vs >=4 RIR; intra-set velocity-loss thresholds (e.g., 10-20% vs 40-50%) as proxies for proximity to failure.
Questions:
a) Shape of the dose-response between proximity to failure and hypertrophy (linear vs non-linear; where gains flatten), contrasted with strength.
b) Does training to failure add hypertrophy when volume is equated vs not equated?
c) Interaction with load: do light loads (<~60% 1RM) require sets closer to failure than heavy loads?
d) Fatigue cost and recovery time after failure vs non-failure sets (label these as non-hypertrophy outcomes).
e) Accuracy of RIR estimation, especially far from failure and in novices, and what this means for prescribing RIR targets.
Specific checks: report the fitted dose-response model and how many effect sizes lie close to failure vs far from failure; note where RIR was estimated rather than measured; keep volume-equated and non-equated analyses separate.
Sub-methods for impact_scoring: velocity-loss thresholds as an autoregulation proxy.
Leads to verify (not pre-approved conclusions):
